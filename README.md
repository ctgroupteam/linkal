# 🌐 linkal.in — Your Branded Store on Your Own Subdomain

> Live Demo: [https://linkalsite.vercel.app](https://linkalsite.vercel.app)  
> Example Customer Store: [https://turkishzaikamenu.vercel.app](https://turkishzaikamenu.vercel.app)

Get a branded website store for your Indian business. Your own subdomain, WhatsApp ordering, AI business analyzer, and Story Studio — live in 10 minutes.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Custom Subdomain**: `yourbrand.linkal.in` with instant edge SSL & zero DNS setup.
- **WhatsApp Ordering**: 1-tap pre-filled order messages sent directly to your WhatsApp.
- **Story Studio**: Generate Instagram-ready product and promo cards in one tap.
- **AI Business Advisor**: Real-time sales insights, best time to post, and trending alerts.
- **Zero Commission**: 100% direct profits via UPI / Cash on delivery.

---

## 👥 Built For Every Vision

- **Instagram Businesses & Boutiques**: Social commerce storefronts with direct WhatsApp checkout.
- **Hotel & Restaurant Digital Menus**: Touchless QR-code menus with instant daily updates.
- **Personal Portfolio Websites**: Showcase creative projects, photography, and case studies.
- **Live CV & Resume Websites**: Stand out to recruiters with an interactive live URL and PDF download.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Frontend**: HTML5, Vanilla CSS (Plus Jakarta Sans & Inter), JavaScript
- **Vector Graphics**: Handcrafted inline SVGs & Lucide Icons
- **Deployment**: Vercel Multi-Tenant Edge Platform
- **DNS & CDN**: Cloudflare Wildcard DNS (`*.linkal.in`)
